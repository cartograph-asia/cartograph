# Decisions

Decisions made while building Cartograph that are not in `CLAUDE.md` or a brief. One line each, newest at the bottom.

## Lead agent

- 2026-11-01 – HEIC uploads are rejected with 400 (JPEG and PNG only); HEIC→JPEG needs libheif via cgo and would rule out a distroless image.
- 2026-11-01 – Work happens on the `prototype` branch with one commit per area.
- 2026-11-01 – The api image is built with `CGO_ENABLED=1` (h3-go wraps the C library) and runs on `gcr.io/distroless/cc-debian12:nonroot`, not scratch.
- 2026-11-01 – `deploy/Caddyfile` is the only Caddyfile; the map pipeline does not ship a snippet.
- 2026-11-01 – Public image URLs are `IMG_BASE_URL/<key>` with `IMG_BASE_URL=http://localhost:8080/img`; Caddy maps `/img/<key>` to `/<bucket>/<key>` on MinIO. *(superseded, see Review follow-ups)*

## Map

- 2026-11-01 – Planetiler runs from `ghcr.io/onthegomap/planetiler:0.10.2` in Docker with `-Xmx4g` (Thailand peaks at ~2.1 GB heap, ~3.5 GB container memory); pmtiles CLI is `protomaps/go-pmtiles:v1.31.2`.
- 2026-11-01 – Planetiler's auxiliary sources live in `map/data/sources/` and its temp dir in `map/data/tmp/`; all of `map/data/` and `*.pmtiles` are git-ignored.
- 2026-11-01 – `--osm-url` is set to the Geofabrik Thailand URL so a missing extract can never silently build Planetiler's default area.
- 2026-11-01 – The Planetiler container runs as the host user so output files belong to the developer.
- 2026-11-01 – The extract's md5 check is best effort with a 30 s timeout (Geofabrik's `.md5` endpoint can hang).
- 2026-11-01 – Glyphs load from the Protomaps basemaps-assets fonts on GitHub Pages (Noto Sans, no key). *(superseded, see Review follow-ups)*
- 2026-11-01 – Labels prefer `name:en`, then `name_en`, `name:latin`, `name`; MapLibre does not shape Thai script well, so English names come first.
- 2026-11-01 – The style adds very low-contrast wood/park fills, buildings from z14 and a dashed country boundary; water names, POIs, road names and house numbers are hidden.
- 2026-11-01 – `/map` and the small maps are flat and north up (Adrian): no drag, touch or keyboard rotation, no pitch (`maxPitch` 0), no compass. Nothing on the map needs a bearing, and a turned view showed the tile archive's edge past the corners of `maxBounds`.
- 2026-11-01 – Air around the countries (Adrian: "more air around the map"). Before, `maxBounds` equalled the archive's bounds (88,-12 – 150,55) at `minZoom` 4.5, so the view stopped exactly at the data's edge, with Timor under the key at the bottom. Now `/map` opens, without a position in the URL, on `HOME` (Southeast and East Asia, 88,-12 – 150,55) whole and centred between masthead and key; it zooms out half a step beyond that view and pans only as far as that view reaches (`minZoom` and `maxBounds` per window, recomputed on resize). A hash or `?cell=` still wins.
- 2026-11-01 – Beyond the archive the map shows the page's paper (`web/src/style.ts`, also on the small maps). Past BOUNDS the archive ends at tile edges that differ by zoom: the sea stops in a straight line and the land-coloured background carries on as land, and land (India, Tibet, Siberia, north Australia) stops just as abruptly. The style's background stays the land colour, because land is implicit in OpenMapTiles and a sea-coloured background would turn all land into sea. A paper fill outside the bounds hides every edge at every zoom, and a blurred line along each edge (48 px wide at zoom 2, 160 px from zoom 7) fades the map into it; land is close to the paper, so mostly the sea thins out. The bounds come from the archive's PMTiles header (fallback 88,-12,150,55), so a rebuild with other BOUNDS needs no client change, and a world archive gets no margin.
- 2026-11-01 – Measured for "more of the world" (test archives from water polygons and Natural Earth only, Planetiler 0.10.2 on the Mac, kept in the session's scratchpad): 88,-12 – 150,55 up to z14 makes 79 MB in 30 s, 60,-25 – 180,65 107 MB in 38 s, the whole world 696 MB in 4 min; a world archive for z0–6 alone is 5 MB (22 s), z0–8 22 MB (28 s). PMTiles stores the identical all-sea tiles once, so a larger BOUNDS costs little; the 46 minutes are the OSM extracts. Recommended to Adrian: `BOUNDS=world` on the next build (about +0.6 GB on 9.3 GB and a few minutes), which removes the edges instead of hiding them; outside Asia the map then shows coast, sea and Natural Earth borders up to z4, without roads or labels. A second, low-zoom world archive would work too, but needs a second URL in `.env` and `/api/config`, a land-coloured cover under the Asia layers, and leaves a seam at the edge.
- 2026-11-01 – The base map covers the whole world (Adrian): `BOUNDS` in `map/build.sh` defaults to `world`. Outside the OSM extracts the archive holds only coasts, sea and Natural Earth borders; PMTiles stores identical sea tiles once, so a test without OSM measured 696 MB for the world against 79 MB for 88,-12 – 150,55, and the full build is expected at about 9.9 GB instead of 9.3 GB with a few minutes more. With it there is no edge left to hide; the paper margin of `web/src/style.ts` stays for archives with a box and disappears by itself for a world archive.
- 2026-11-01 – The plot grid is drawn everywhere on the map, not only over Thailand (Adrian); this replaces the Thailand bounding box of the Frontend entry above as a drawing limit. `GET /api/config` adds `surveyable`, the box around every imported province (`db.SurveyableBounds`); the map asks for plots and coverage only for cells within half a degree of it, draws blank plots and groups elsewhere, and says "Plots here cannot be surveyed yet." there. Blank groups outside the surveyable area appear from zoom 6.5 and only while they fit in `MAX_VIEW_CELLS` (on a desktop window over China that is from about zoom 7); below that the map draws groups only where there is data, as blank groups are invisible there anyway. The box grows by itself with every imported country.
- 2026-11-01 – `/map` and `/map/` answer with `Cache-Control: no-cache` and the hashed files under `/map/assets/` with `public, max-age=31536000, immutable`. Without a header, browsers cached the page heuristically and kept loading the old bundle after a rebuild.

## Backend

- 2026-11-01 – HEIC is detected by the ISO-BMFF `ftyp` brand (heic/heix/hevc/hevx/heim/heis/hevm/hevs/mif1/msf1).
- 2026-11-01 – Coverage uses `plots.parent5` / `plots.parent6` columns (migration 0002), set on insert and indexed; 0001 matches `CLAUDE.md` exactly.
- 2026-11-01 – The plot row is upserted before the photo row in the same transaction (photo UUID generated in Go); `claimed` comes from `RETURNING (xmax = 0)`.
- 2026-11-01 – `claimed_at`, `uploaded_at` and the key's `yyyy/mm` all use the server's upload time in UTC.
- 2026-11-01 – `taken_at` is DateTimeOriginal adjusted by OffsetTimeOriginal; without an offset the wall-clock time is stored as UTC.
- 2026-11-01 – EXIF GPS wins over form `lat`/`lng`; GPS exactly at 0,0 counts as missing; a lone or out-of-range `lat`/`lng` is a 400.
- 2026-11-01 – EXIF is not read from PNGs, so PNG uploads always need `lat`/`lng`.
- 2026-11-01 – Images above 100 megapixels are rejected before decoding; at most two images are processed at once.
- 2026-11-01 – Transparent PNGs are flattened onto white before JPEG encoding; `thumb` is JPEG q80.
- 2026-11-01 – `surveyor` is trimmed, required, and at most 100 characters.
- 2026-11-01 – The multipart body is held in memory (no temp files on distroless); the body limit is `MAX_UPLOAD_MB` + 1 MB.
- 2026-11-01 – The `thumb` in `GET /api/plots` is the claiming photo's thumbnail.
- 2026-11-01 – An empty `cells` parameter returns an empty list; cells are de-duplicated and lower-cased.
- 2026-11-01 – Stored objects carry `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable` (keys never change).
- 2026-11-01 – The api checks that the bucket exists at start-up and only logs a warning if it does not; it never creates it.
- 2026-11-01 – `/healthz` is liveness only and does not ping the database.

## Security

Fixes from the security review of 2026-11-01 that matter already in alpha.

- 2026-11-01 – Exact photo coordinates never leave the database, not even rounded: `photos.lat`/`lng` are stored for the record, and everything public (pages, feeds, JSON, map links, bounds) is computed from the plot's H3 cell. The Surveyor page's bounds and its "Open on the map" link come from the boundaries of the Surveyor's plots (`cellBounds` in `internal/db/views.go`); before, they were the box around the photo positions, which put a single photo within about 11 m. The API returns cells only, the RSS feeds carry no geo elements, and the plot page's small map is centred on the cell.
- 2026-11-01 – Decompression bombs: `photos.Process` checks the header against a pixel limit per format before decoding: JPEG 60 MP, PNG 30 MP, PNG with 16 bits per channel 15 MP (the same memory as 30 MP at 8 bits), replacing the single 100 MP limit under which a 757 KB 16-bit PNG decoded to 908 MB. Measured peaks at the new limits: 166 MB (JPEG), 195 MB (PNG), 184 MB (16-bit PNG), 394 MB for a 60 MP JPEG with an EXIF rotation. 16-bit PNGs are accepted within their limit rather than refused, since some photo editors export them.
- 2026-11-01 – Uploads (Adrian, PR #6): `POST /api/photos` spools the body to a temporary file (`os.CreateTemp`, removed on every path) without a processing slot, so a slow upload holds disk, not memory, and blocks nobody. The slot (`MaxConcurrentProcessing`, still 2) is held only while the photo is read into memory and decoded, and given back before the variants are stored. Each Surveyor has one upload in progress at a time; a second gets 429 "another upload of yours is still in progress" (a first step towards H1). The route sets its own read and write deadlines (`api.DefaultUploadTimeout`, 10 minutes: 20 MB at about 300 kbit/s over Thai mobile data); the server's 2-minute timeouts stay for every other route.
- 2026-11-01 – Every long-running container has a `mem_limit` in `deploy/docker-compose.yml`, set from `.env` (`API_MEM_LIMIT` 1536m, `POSTGRES_MEM_LIMIT` 1g, `GARAGE_MEM_LIMIT` 512m, `CADDY_MEM_LIMIT` 256m, `WEB_MEM_LIMIT` 128m), and the api runs with `GOMEMLIMIT` (`API_GOMEMLIMIT`, 1GiB). Out of memory, the kernel then stops the container that overran instead of picking a process on the host. The one-shot imports have no limit. Values to be checked against the Hetzner host's size in the deployment brief.
- 2026-11-01 – Caddy's access log replaces the `token` query parameter with `REDACTED` (`format filter`, `request>uri query`), so magic links never reach the log; the api logs paths only. The confirm form posts the token in the body, and its page sends no referrer.
- 2026-11-01 – Sign-in links are written to the log only with an explicit `MAIL_DEV_LOG=1` (Adrian, PR #6): without `SMTP_HOST` and without it the api refuses to start, on http and https alike, and with an https `BASE_URL` it requires `SMTP_HOST` even with `MAIL_DEV_LOG=1`. The check runs in `loadConfig`, before the database is touched. `deploy/.env.example` sets `MAIL_DEV_LOG=1` for development; `docker-compose.yml` passes it through without a default, so a local `deploy/.env` needs the line (it is the one variable without a compose default).
- 2026-11-01 – `golang.org/x/image` moves from the 2019 version that `disintegration/imaging` pins to v0.47.0, `x/net` to v0.62.0 and `x/crypto` to v0.58.0; `govulncheck ./...` reports no reachable vulnerability (one advisory in `x/crypto` has no fix and is not called). `imaging` itself is unmaintained; replacing it is not part of this fix.
- 2026-11-01 – Pixel limits stay at 60 MP (JPEG), 30 MP (PNG) and 15 MP (16-bit PNG), and 16-bit PNGs are accepted within their limit (Adrian, PR #6). The memory limits stay as set until the deployment brief sizes them for the Hetzner host (about 16 GB including swap). `disintegration/imaging` stays for now; replacing it (likely with `golang.org/x/image/draw`) is a separate task.
- 2026-11-01 – Crawlers are kept out during alpha (Adrian): `CRAWLERS=block` (default) or `allow` in `.env`, anything else stops the api. `internal/crawlers` is the one source of `/robots.txt` (no static file, nothing in Caddy). With `block`, robots.txt disallows `/` for `*` and every response of the api carries `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow, noarchive`, set by the outermost middleware and set again before the header is written, so a handler's own weaker tag (the admin guard's `noindex`) cannot loosen it; Caddy adds the same header to the map app, tiles and photos through an `expression` matcher on `{$CRAWLERS:block}` (anything but `allow` blocks). With `allow`, robots.txt disallows `/settings`, `/signin` and `/api/` only and no blanket header is sent; `/admin` stays unnamed as in the admin brief. In both modes the AI and SEO crawlers in `crawlers.AICrawlers` get `Disallow: /`; whether the beta lets AI crawlers in is open for Adrian. Photos or tiles served from another host (`IMG_BASE_URL`, `TILES_URL` in production) need the header set there.
