# About

Cartograph is a photo survey of Southeast and East Asia, made by the people who live and travel there.

The land is divided into plots of about 0.74 km² each. A Surveyor takes a photo inside a plot and uploads it. The first photo claims the plot, and its Surveyor stays its first surveyor for good. Everyone who comes later adds to the plot's story: another season, another street, the bridge after it was rebuilt. More in [About the plots](/about/plots).

Coverage is the share of a region's plots that have at least one photo. We start with Thailand.

## How to survey

1. Open the [map](/map) and find a blank plot near you.
2. Take a photo there. The plot is read from the photo's location.
3. Sign in with your e-mail address (there is no password) and upload it.

That is all. There are no streaks to keep and nothing to buy. Every photo you upload is published under [CC BY-SA 4.0](/licence), so the survey stays open for everyone.

## Where the idea comes from

[Geograph Britain and Ireland](https://www.geograph.org.uk/) has collected photos of every corner of those islands since 2005. Cartograph tries the same for Asia, on a map built from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/).

## Who runs it

Cartograph is a small, independent project. It follows a short list of [principles](/principles): people first, newest first, no tracking, no growth pressure. The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/cartograph-asia). Write to [hello@cartograph.asia](mailto:hello@cartograph.asia).
