## Visiting the site

Reading Cartograph needs no account and sets no cookie. The pages and the map load nothing from other companies: fonts, map tiles and photos all come from cartograph.asia itself.

Our web server logs each request: the time, the address requested, your IP address, your browser's name and the page you came from. We keep these logs **7 days** and then delete them. We use them to keep the site running and to find abuse (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Links that sign you in are blanked out in the logs.

We count page views from these logs with Matomo, which runs on our own server: no script on the pages, no cookie, and nothing goes to another company. Account and admin pages are not counted.

## Your account

If you are invited and sign in, we store:

- **your e-mail address**, to send you sign-in links (there are no passwords) and nothing else; it is never shown on the site;
- **a session** (a cookie named `cg_session` and its record on our server), so that you stay signed in; it ends after 30 days without a visit, or when you sign out;
- **your Surveyor name, display name, profile text and home region**, which are public on your profile.

We process this to provide the account you asked for (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR).

When someone without an invitation asks to sign in, we send one short e-mail saying that Cartograph is invitation-only, and keep only a one-way hash of the address for a day, so that we do not send it twice.

Sign-in e-mails are sent through mailbox.org (Heinlein Hosting GmbH, Berlin) as our e-mail provider.

## Your photos

A photo you upload is **public** under the licence CC BY-SA 4.0 (see [Data & licence](/licence)), with your Surveyor name and the time you uploaded it. Before storing it we remove its metadata (EXIF) except what we need: where and when it was taken. We store the exact position, but the site only ever shows which plot (about 0.74 km²) the photo is in, never the exact spot.

Please do not upload photos in which people can be recognised without their consent.

## Where the data is

Everything runs on our own server at Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany (processor under Art. 28 GDPR). Backups are encrypted and kept on the operator's own computer in Germany.

## How long

Your account and photos stay until you ask us to delete them. Logs: 7 days. Backups of the database: up to 12 months.

## Your rights

You can ask what we store about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, and receive your data in a common format (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Write to [hello@cartograph.asia](mailto:hello@cartograph.asia). Photos you published under CC BY-SA may already have been copied by others under that licence; we delete them from Cartograph.
