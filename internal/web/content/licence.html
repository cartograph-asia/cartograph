Each photo page links to the full-size image and states its licence.

Map and place names

The base map, the place names and the province boundaries come from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL). Plots are cells of the open H3 grid at resolution 8.

Feeds and export

There is an RSS feed of the latest surveys, and one for every region and every Surveyor, linked from their pages. A full data export is planned; until then, write to hello@cartograph.asia and we will send you one.

Privacy

Nothing on Cartograph needs an account to view. There is no tracking and no advertising, so there is no cookie banner. Signing in sets one cookie to keep you signed in. From each photo we keep its location and the time it was taken, and remove all other metadata before publishing it.
